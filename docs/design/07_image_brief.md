# Image brief — one cover image per industry

For the owner to generate in ChatGPT (or to choose from a licensed photo library). Paste the **shared style** first,
then one industry's subject line. Generate one at a time and keep the ones that look like they belong to the same set.

## Where each image is used

Each image appears in up to three places, so it has to survive very different crops:

| Place | Shape of the crop | What that means for the picture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage carousel card | Wide strip, about 2.3 : 1 | The whole width shows; top and bottom are trimmed |
| Dashboard banner (inside the demo) | Very wide, thin strip; the left half sits under the text | Keep the subject in the **middle band** of the height and **slightly right of centre** |
| Optometry dashboard only (new design, awaiting approval) | Close to square, beside the patient-flow chart | The subject must still read when cropped to a square around it |

## Technical requirements

- **Landscape**, the widest format the tool offers (3 : 2 or 16 : 9), at least 1500 px wide.
- Main subject in the **middle band** of the height and slightly **right of centre**; calmer, darker space on the left.
- No text, logos, signage, brand names, number plates or watermarks.
- No recognisable faces: people only from behind, as hands, or out of focus. **No children at all** (the school image is an empty classroom).
- Nothing that looks like a real, identifiable business or place.
- File names exactly: `optometry`, `medical`, `automotive`, `tyres`, `mining`, `construction`, `realestate`, `legal`,
  `security`, `cleaning`, `education`, `mall`, `hr`, `recruitment` (JPG or PNG; I resize and compress them).

## Shared style (paste this first)

> Cinematic editorial photograph, realistic, natural directional light, shallow depth of field, clean and premium,
> cool neutral colour palette with soft blue-grey tones and one warm highlight. Wide landscape composition with the
> main subject slightly right of centre and calm, darker negative space on the left. No text, no logos, no signage,
> no watermarks, no recognisable faces. Not an illustration, not a 3D render.
